# Academy artwork integration status

The academy now contains **ten original CI/DIO educational illustrations**, recovered from user-generated artwork and permanently imported into `assets/original-art/`. Their source transfers were **Canva-generated 200×112 previews**, resized with high-quality resampling for **module thumbnail use only**. The files are not true high-resolution originals, so they should not be advertised as HD.

The academy also includes eleven higher-resolution, reused illustrations from the owner's Social Services project as interim larger scene imagery in `assets/scene/`. These are references, not newly commissioned CI-specific scenes.

The source Canva image preview links used to import the ten thumbnails were temporary. The one-time importer and its signed URLs have been removed from the repository; the locally committed images remain available.

Full-resolution original images, a complete 66-lesson unique artwork collection, video verification, and final visual QA are still open requirements. All authored clinical educational material requires technical approval before formal institutional deployment.
